About this app

Perioperative & ICU Calculator brings together formulas, scores and conversions used in perioperative and intensive care. It shows only numbers, comparisons with criteria stated in the original sources or package inserts, and the sources; it does not make diagnostic or treatment decisions. Each calculator has a “Formula & basis” view showing the formula, scope and sources. The app is available in Japanese and English.

The dosing calculators and composition totals are also available on the web (drhac-gh.github.io/periop-calc). The web version has no ads.

FAQ

Are the values I enter sent anywhere?
No. All calculations run on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where do the drug product values come from?
From Japanese package inserts (PMDA). Each product shows its brand name, marketing authorization holder and revision date. Values may change when an insert is revised, and products in other countries may differ.
