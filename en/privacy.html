Perioperative & ICU Calculator (the “App”) performs calculations for formulas, scores and conversions used in perioperative and intensive care. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Provider

App: Perioperative & ICU Calculator (Japanese name: 周術期・集中治療計算ツール)
Provider: Dr. Hac
Contact: drhac.jp@gmail.com

2. Information the App handles

The App does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and has no accounts. It handles the following information.

The App does not collect patient information, precise location, contacts, photos or health data, and does not use analytics tools.

Please do not enter information that identifies a patient, such as a name. Do not write patient information or calculation values in the details field of “Report a problem” either.

To show new-version notices, the App reads the current released version number from Firebase (no information about you is sent).

Advertising

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob. On iOS, it asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) the first time you use it. If you do not allow tracking, ads that do not use the advertising identifier and are not personalized are shown. The calculators work the same either way.

  • How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can change tracking permission at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and the Android advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Web version

The web version (drhac-gh.github.io/periop-calc) runs calculations only in your browser and never sends the values you enter. It has no ads. Pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which handles access logs.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell any information. Except where disclosure is required by law, the following companies handle information for the purposes above.

  • Google LLC (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore): anonymous sign-in, storing problem reports, providing the released version number
  • Google LLC (AdMob): advertising information to deliver ads

4. Deleting information

Favorites, recently used calculators and the language setting are all removed when you delete the App from your device. To request deletion of your anonymous user ID or problem reports you sent, contact us at the address above. For advertising information, see Google’s page above.

5. Audience

The App is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed to children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy as needed. Important changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
